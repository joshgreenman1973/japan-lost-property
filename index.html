Every item Tokyo police processed, by what became of it

Under Japan's Lost Property Act an unclaimed item belongs to whoever handed it in, if they claim it within two months. Anything nobody wants becomes the property of the Metropolitan Government. Sorted by the share that found its owner again.

Official lost-property search, by prefecture

Japan's National Police Agency keeps an index of every prefectural lost-property search. Most prefectures share one national database; a few run their own. Items of value are listed across prefectures: cash over ¥10,000, securities, goods worth ¥10,000 or more, identity documents, bank cards and mobile phones. Most of these are Japanese-language only.
